Questions

      
Frequently asked questions

      

        
Does online rehab really work?
For most sports and orthopaedic injuries, yes. Recovery depends on doing the right exercises consistently. I design your program from your reports and your goal, check your technique by video, and adjust it as you improve.

        
Do I need an MRI or X-ray first?
Not always. Send what you have. If I think you need a scan or a doctor's review before starting, I'll tell you.

        
How long is a program?
It depends on the injury and your goal. Most programs run several weeks, with regular check-ins. I'll give you a clear timeline after reviewing your case.

        
Can I have in-person sessions?
Message me on WhatsApp and we'll arrange what suits your case best.

        
How do I pay?
Only after we agree on your program, through InstaPay. Details are below.

Professional profile

        
Dr. Abdelrahman Noureldin

        
Physiotherapist, MSc · Orthopaedic & Sports Rehabilitation

        
Dr. Abdelrahman Noureldin is a physiotherapist specialising in orthopaedic and sports rehabilitation. He holds a Bachelor's degree in Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation from Eastern Mediterranean University and a Master of Science in Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation from the European University of Lefke, where his research examined the relationship between core stability, agility and knee injuries in football players.

        
He worked as a physiotherapist for Larnaka Gençler Birliği Football Club, managing player injuries, injury prevention programs and return-to-play decisions. His clinical practice combines thorough assessment, manual therapy and evidence-based exercise prescription, with close follow-up until each patient reaches their functional goal.
