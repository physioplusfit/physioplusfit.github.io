What I treat

Rehabilitation for the injuries that stop you moving

Sports injuries

Muscle strains, ligament sprains and overuse injuries, with a staged return to training and competition.

After surgery

ACL reconstruction, meniscus repair, fractures and joint surgery, from the first week to full function.

Back & neck pain

Disc problems, posture-related pain and stiffness, treated with manual therapy and targeted exercise.

Joint problems

Knee, shoulder, hip and ankle pain, restoring range of motion, strength and confidence in daily life.

Injury prevention

Screening and conditioning programs for athletes and teams to lower the risk of injury and re-injury.

Remote programs & follow-up

A structured home program with regular check-ins, wherever you are.

Professional profile

        
Dr. Abdelrahman Ayman

        
Physiotherapist, MSc · Orthopaedic & Sports Rehabilitation

        
Dr. Abdelrahman Ayman is a physiotherapist specialising in orthopaedic and sports rehabilitation. He holds a Bachelor's degree in Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation from Eastern Mediterranean University and a Master of Science in Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation from the European University of Lefke, where his research examined the relationship between core stability, agility and knee injuries in football players.

        
He worked as a physiotherapist for Larnaka Gençler Birliği Football Club, managing player injuries, injury prevention programs and return-to-play decisions. His clinical practice combines thorough assessment, manual therapy and evidence-based exercise prescription, with close follow-up until each patient reaches their functional goal.
